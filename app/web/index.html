WoofDecoder

Upload a clip of a dog. The app finds every bark, growl or whine and makes a best guess at the situation behind it, using three clues: how it sounds, the dog's body language, and the surroundings.

WoofDecoder estimates the likely context of barking from patterns in sound and video. It does not translate dog language. Some models and datasets are licensed for non-commercial use only; this project is for academic, non-commercial use.
